Motocross RPG:
The Road to Legend

Build your rider. Build your bike. Make your name in motocross. Start as an unknown rider with a broken 50cc and work your way up, one race day at a time.

Play the alpha in your browser Help make it Join the discussion
This is an early alpha, made as a hobby. Most of the art is placeholder, there's no music yet, and there will be bugs. Your career saves in the browser you play in, and updates can change things. It's free and offered as-is, with no release dates promised.

What you can play today

The whole early career loop works now: earn money, keep the bike going, race, level up, repeat.

Start from nothing

A broken 50cc, a little money and a mechanic in Home Town who gets you going.

Earn your way

Work shifts in the Industrial Zone making frame brackets and pistons.

Look after the bike

Every lap wears its parts. Buy better grades, repair, fit spares.

Race three venues

Farmland MX, Riverside Arena and Longmoor Enduro: two motos against 15 riders.

Win the badge

The Local Champion Championship: three rounds, qualifying and points for the Area 1 badge.

Build your own tracks

A track editor in the browser, and your tracks race in the game.

Help wanted: artists and musicians

The game needs a proper look and a sound of its own. If you'd like to volunteer and see your work in a motocross game, say hello. This is an unpaid hobby project, so there's no budget, but every contributor is credited in the game and here.

Introduce yourself in Discussions

Share a link to some of your work and what you'd like to make. Please only offer work that's yours to give, and say what licence it's under (for example CC BY 4.0) so it can be used in the game.

Talk about the game

The Discussions board is the game's community page: share screenshots and tracks you've built, suggest ideas, and follow announcements for each new build. You need a free GitHub account to post.
