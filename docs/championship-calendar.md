# Championship calendar

Every championship in the game's plan, from Boss's championship planner (4 Nov 2026). Each area unlocks through the story, opens some new championships, then has its Local Champion and its area cup. The vets championships open with the 40+, 50+ and 60+ Vets classes (rider levels 80, 90 and 95), and the Ultimate Event at level 99.

Naming rules (Boss, 4 Nov 2026): area cups are named after bike parts; the rest are named after where they race, with the discipline at the end: MX, EN (enduro), SE (sprint enduro) and SCMX (sidecarcross) are Championships, SX (Supercross) and EX (EnduroCross) are Series, and Takedowns go by their own names.

Only Area 1 is in the game so far, in `public/data/championships.json` (Home Town Local Champion and the Sprocket Cup). The rest go in as their areas' venues are built. Rounds are races at each venue; the area numbers are the areas those venues are in.
